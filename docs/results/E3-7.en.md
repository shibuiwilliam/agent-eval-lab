# E3-7 Escape-defect rate, ε-exploration and the inviolable set

*日本語版: [E3-7.md](E3-7.md)*

## The five items
- Hypothesis: At a 40% budget the escape-defect rate is ≤ 0.1. With ε=0.05 a random test is mixed in every time. The inviolable set is always selected regardless of budget
- Planted condition: Comparing full-execution results against selection results across all changes
- Control: A 100% budget → escape-defect rate 0
- Pass criteria: escape_rate <= 0.1, random_included >= 1, inviolable_rate == 1.0, full_budget_escape == 0
- Provenance: simulated

## Method
We selected at budget ratios of 5% / 20% / 40% / 100% and measured the escape-defect rate (the
fraction of unselected tests that actually flipped), the number of random tests mixed in by
ε-exploration, and the selection rate of the inviolable set (`risk: inviolable`) (report 3.8).
The inviolable set is implemented to be selected outside the selection logic, so we confirmed
it is at 100% even at a 5% budget. Flips follow the ADR-009 definition.
The control is a 100% budget (run everything → escape-defect rate 0).

- The inviolable set (T-005, T-011, T-025) is implemented to be included unconditionally, outside the selection logic. It is selected 100% of the time even at a 5% budget.
- ε-exploration mixes in at least one test regardless of budget. At a 100% budget everything is already selected and there is no room to mix, so the count reported is the minimum over the conditions with budget < 100%.
- The escape-defect rate of ≤ 0.1 could not be met. The cause is the same as E3-3: the redundancy penalty (skip similarity > 0.8 over tool-name sequences) treats tasks of the same kind inside the candidate set as mutual duplicates and spends the budget on diverse tasks outside the candidate set. The escape-defect rate of the variant with redundancy switched off is reported alongside.
- **Corrected diagnosis (confirmed live, ADR-014)**: this effect occurs because sim trajectories collapse to nearly identical tool-name sequences across tasks. In live trajectories, 10 tasks split into 9 distinct sequences and only 1 of 45 pairs (2%) exceeds a similarity of 0.8. The definition of the redundancy penalty is not changed (the same correction as in E3-3).
- The verdict is FAIL (a flaw in the experiment design). The inviolable-set guarantee (100% selection even at a 5% budget) and the zero escape-defect rate at a 100% budget do hold.
