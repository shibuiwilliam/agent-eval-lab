## Method
From v01_baseline's repeats in the corpus (10 of them) we computed pass@k and pass^k per task
using the formulas in report 4.10. Boundary tasks are those with a v01 pass rate between 0.3
and 0.7; trivial tasks are T-901 / T-902.
Step-count variance comes from the repeats of v01 and v04_loopy.
pass@k is "the probability of succeeding at least once in k draws" and pass^k is "the
probability of succeeding in all k draws"; the larger the gap, the more the test is one that
"works sometimes".

## Findings and limitations
- Boundary tasks: ['T-001', 'T-003', 'T-004', 'T-006', 'T-007', 'T-008', 'T-009', 'T-010', 'T-015', 'T-016', 'T-017', 'T-018', 'T-019', 'T-020', 'T-021', 'T-022', 'T-026', 'T-029', 'T-202', 'T-402', 'T-404']
- Trivial tasks (T-901 / T-902) pass on every repeat, so pass@3 = pass^3 = 1.0 and the gap is 0. On boundary tasks pass@3 climbs close to 1.0 while pass^3 stays low — a large gap between "draw 3 times and one will pass" and "all 3 pass".
- In sim mode a given (task, version, repeat) is deterministic, but the "luck" changes across repeats, producing variation between repeats. That stands in for live sampling variance and is not necessarily drawn from the same distribution as live variance.
